# Domain context

## Medication tracking

- **Medicine:** A catalog entry describing a medicine and its usual dose and unit.
- **Medication schedule:** A user's recurring instruction for when and how much of a medicine to take.
- **Dose occurrence:** One planned dose at a particular local date and time.
- **Medicine intake:** A record that a dose was actually taken. An intake may match a scheduled dose or stand alone.
- **Reminder:** A notification about a dose occurrence. It is not evidence that the dose was taken.
- **Unrecorded:** No intake is linked to an occurrence. This does not establish that the dose was not taken.
