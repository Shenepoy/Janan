SHENEPOY · JANAN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Janan (الجَنَان), developed by Shenepoy, is a health diary for blood pressure, weight, medicines and related records. This policy covers the Janan app, Janan Plus account features and this website.

Your local diary works without an account. Local-only use does not upload your diary to Janan servers. Account features are optional and their availability depends on the version and service settings.

Information you choose to record

Your device stores the measurements, pulse, weight, notes, medicine schedules and dose history you enter or import. Depending on the features you use, this may also include body-profile details, symptoms, sleep, activity, lab observations and other health context. Preferences and saved Bluetooth-device identifiers are stored locally.

Device permissions and Health Connect

  • Bluetooth / Nearby devices: connects to monitors and scales you choose. Older Android versions require location permission for Bluetooth discovery; Janan does not use it to track your location.
  • Health Connect: with your permission, reads and writes blood-pressure and weight records, including older records when history access is granted. You can revoke permissions in Health Connect. Its data is used for your health-diary features, not advertising.
  • Notifications and alarms: deliver reminders you configure. Notification settings control what appears on your device.
  • Files: lets you choose records to import and destinations for exports. Exported files are separate copies under your control.

Optional account and cloud features

If you create a Janan Plus account, the authentication service processes your sign-in information, such as your email address and account identifier. When cloud sync is available and you enable it, the records in that account's diary are sent to our service for synchronization and backups. Signing out does not delete the account or its separate local database.

When available, sharing sends only the report or categories you select to your chosen recipients. Anyone with an unprotected report link can open it until it expires or is revoked. Revocation cannot recall copies already saved by recipients. Connected applications require their own authorization and can be disconnected.

Hosted AI, voice, scanning and paid checkout are not currently available for general use. These disabled features do not send your diary to an AI provider. Before activating them, we will update the relevant disclosures to identify the processors and data involved and obtain the required choices from you.

Providers and website requests

Google Firebase Hosting serves this website and the web app. Supabase provides optional account authentication, database storage and server functions. Requests to online services necessarily expose connection information, including an IP address and browser or app request details, to the service providers. Online service providers may process information outside your country.

Where optional usage analytics is available and you opt in, Janan aggregates a small set of feature-success and failure counts before forwarding them to PostHog. Health values, notes, medicine names and account identifiers are not included in those aggregate events. Janan does not use session replay or advertising trackers. These static policy pages contain no analytics scripts.

Use, sharing and security

Information is used to provide the features you choose, authenticate your account, respond to support requests and protect the service. We do not sell your health data or use it for advertising. Data may be processed by the providers needed to operate those features, shared at your direction, or disclosed where legally required.

Online connections use HTTPS. Cloud access is restricted to authorized accounts and explicitly authorized sharing. Synced information is not end-to-end encrypted: the service can process it to provide enabled features. Device and browser security protect local storage; protect your device and exported files.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them or clear the app's storage. Cloud records remain until you delete them or your account; individual backups and reports also have expiry rules. Deleting a cloud account removes its live diary records, stored AI history and account backups, and revokes sharing access. Minimal security/audit records may remain with the deleted account reference removed. Provider recovery backups and operational logs may retain residual data until their configured retention periods expire; these are separate from the account backups deleted by Janan.

We do not promise immediate erasure of every provider backup or recipient copy. Contact us for the retention details applicable to your request. See Delete your account and data for the available controls and private request channel.

Your choices and contact

You can use the local diary without an account, export your records, delete local data, revoke device permissions, turn off optional consent settings and request deletion of a cloud account. Turning off sync or signing out is not an account-deletion request. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided account information can contact us for help with removal.

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email shenepoy@gmail.com. Do not send health records, passwords or verification codes. We may ask for limited information to verify account ownership. Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated date.
