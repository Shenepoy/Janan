Delete your account and data

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This page is for Janan (الجَنَان), developed by Shenepoy, including optional Janan Plus accounts.

No account? No account-deletion request is needed. The local diary works without registration. Follow the local-data steps below.

Request cloud-account deletion by email

You can request deletion without reinstalling the app or having an active subscription.

Email an account-deletion request

  1. Email shenepoy@gmail.com, preferably from the address used for your Janan account.
  2. Use the subject Janan account deletion request and state that you want your account and associated cloud data deleted.
  3. If you cannot access the original email, say so. We will arrange an ownership check before deleting anything.

Send only the information needed to identify the account. Do not include health records, passwords or verification codes, and do not post account details in a public GitHub issue. We will reply about verification, completion and any information that must be retained.

Delete an account from the app

If you are signed in and account controls are available, open Janan Plus → Cloud & privacy → Delete cloud account, read the confirmation and confirm deletion. Export anything you want to keep first. If this option is unavailable, use the email request above.

What is deleted, and what remains?

  • Deleted: the sign-in account, its live cloud health records, account backups and stored AI history, if any.
  • Revoked: report links, caregiver access and connected-app access associated with the deleted account.
  • Separate copies: local device databases, exported files and copies saved by recipients are not remotely erased. Remove them separately.
  • Limited residual records: security/audit records can remain with the deleted account reference removed. Service-provider recovery backups and logs expire under the providers' configured retention schedules. We can explain any retained information and applicable period when handling your request.

Deleting an account does not cancel a store subscription. If you have one, cancel it separately in the store where you subscribed.

Delete local data

In Janan, open Settings → Data → Delete. Choose the categories you want to remove and confirm. This includes measurements, notes, weight, medicine history, reminder schedules and available health tools. Settings can be reset separately. For a synced diary, changes may propagate to the cloud and your other devices when sync is enabled.

To remove all local app storage, use Android's App info → Storage → Clear storage, or clear this site's data in your browser. This removes local diaries and preferences on that device, including unsynced changes; it does not delete a cloud account. Delete downloaded exports and backups separately. Manage copies held in Health Connect through its own data controls.
